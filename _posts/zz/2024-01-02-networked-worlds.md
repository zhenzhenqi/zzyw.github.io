Research Memo chapter, published by Co-matter in collaboration with WeTransfer, 2024.

Free downloadable 60-page PDF zine hosted on the co—matter website at [https://co-matter.com/project/networked-worlds](https://co-matter.com/project/networked-worlds). 

Networked Worlds is the third part of Networked Culture, a series of publications created in collaboration with WeTransfer that explore the effects of networked technologies on the creative process. Explores "worlding" as a creative strategy to prototype alternative futures across emerging technologies like AI, 3D engines, and collective software systems.

Idea & creative direction: Severin Matusek

Research & production: Kesia Inkersole, Nelya Rosa

Art director: Paloma Moniz

3D Design: Lucy Hardcastle Studio
